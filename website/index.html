LIGHTWEIGHT SCREENSHOT TOOL FOR WINDOWS

Capture anything. Make it clear. Share it instantly.

Meet darkshot — a lightweight screenshot companion that helps you capture, annotate, and share your screen without interrupting your workflow.

Download for Windows Explore Features

Windows 11 · 64-bit · Rust-powered

  • Lightweight and native

    Written in Rust for Windows. A single portable executable, no installer.

  • Quiet in your tray

  • Keyboard-first

A

Smart screenshot capture

Click to capture a window. Drag to capture any region.

  • Drag to capture any custom region of the screen.
  • Hover a window and click once, or press Enter, to capture exactly that window.
  • Works across multiple monitors and mixed display DPI settings.
  • Optionally remembers where your last selection was.
  • Optionally includes the mouse pointer in the image.

Window selection only reads each window's position and size, never its title or contents.

  • T Text
  • N Numbered markers
  • V Select and edit
  • [] Four stroke & text sizes
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y Multi-step undo / redo
  • 48 colours plus your own

With the Select tool you can move, resize, recolour or delete any shape you drew.

C

Privacy redaction

Share screenshots without exposing sensitive information.

Drag the Blur tool (B) over anything private. It isn't a Gaussian blur: darkshot covers the area with an opaque solid colour sampled from the image, so the text underneath is not kept in the exported screenshot.

  • Sharp-edged cover, no soft blur that hints at the original.
  • Hold Shift for a perfect square.
  • Applies to the saved, copied and uploaded image alike.

Tip: redactions are burned in when you export. If you move or delete one before exporting, what was underneath is visible again.

E

Cloudflare R2 integration

Your screenshots. Your storage. Your control.

  • Connect your own Cloudflare R2 bucket in Options.
  • Images upload straight to it and the public link is copied for you.
  • Upload as PNG, or as JPEG with quality 50–100.
  • Choose link expiry: 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or never.

R2 is required only for cloud uploads. Saving and copying work without it. Cloudflare R2 is your own Cloudflare account and service; darkshot doesn't provide or host storage.

Expiry works through lifecycle rules you create once in Cloudflare (d1/, d7/, d30/); darkshot can't create or verify them. The README walks through it.

F

Screenshot history

Your recent captures, always within reach.

  • Off by default. Turn it on in Options.
  • Keeps your 50 most recent items, stored locally on your PC.
  • Thumbnails and a preview for each item.
  • Search by file name, date, type or link; filter Saved, Uploaded or Copied.
  • Open link, copy image, copy link, pin or remove entries.

Removing an entry never deletes the file you saved or the image on R2.

G

Pin to screen

Keep a screenshot in view while you work.

  • Pin a selection from the overlay with the Pin button or Ctrl+P. It lands exactly where you selected, with your annotations.
  • Or pin any item from the Screenshot history with its Pin button.
  • Each pin is its own window, always on top. Drag it anywhere.
  • Scroll to zoom around the pointer; Ctrl+scroll to change opacity.
  • Right-click for a menu (copy, save, recognize text, zoom, opacity, close); Esc closes a pin.
  • Up to 32 pins at once.
  • While pins are open, the tray menu offers "Close all pinned images (n)".

Pins stay on your PC: pinning never uses the network.

H

Text recognition

Copy just the lines you need, not the whole page.

  • Select a region and press Ctrl+T or the Recognize text button. A pinned image has Recognize text in its right-click menu.
  • The Recognized text window shows your image with a box around each line, next to the text you can edit.
  • Click a box to copy that line. Ctrl+click to pick several lines; they are copied in reading order. Click empty space to clear the selection.
  • On the image: ↑/↓ move between lines, Enter copies the line, Space adds or removes it, Ctrl+A picks all, Esc clears the selection, then closes.
  • Three switches, off by default and remembered: Single line, Separate paragraphs, Copy automatically when done.
  • Copy all copies the whole text and keeps the window open. Up to 8 text windows at once.

Runs on your PC with Windows' built-in OCR and the languages installed in Windows: no network, no history entry. Windows has no Vietnamese recognizer yet, so accented letters may lose their marks.

I

Step capture & scrolling capture

Document a process, or capture a page taller than your screen.

  • Step capture: start a session from the tray menu. A small bar stays on top with Capture step, Review and Finish. Up to 99 steps.
  • While a session is open, Ctrl+N in the overlay adds the selection, with its annotations, as a step.
  • Reorder steps, add a note to each, delete or preview them, then export a darkshot-steps-… folder with 01.png, 02.png… and steps.md. The session survives a restart.
  • Scrolling capture: select a region, press Ctrl+L or the Scrolling capture button, then scroll the content down slowly yourself. darkshot joins the parts into one tall image, up to 30,000 px.
  • Joins darkshot is unsure about are flagged so you can check the image. The result window offers Save, Copy, Upload and Pin.

Scrolling capture is vertical only, inside the fixed region you chose, and isn't guaranteed for every app; darkshot never sends scroll input to other apps. Step images never go into history. Both run on your PC with no network.

PRIVACY & ARCHITECTURE

Your screen. Your data. Your control.

Screenshots can be sensitive, so here is exactly what stays on your PC and what leaves it.

On your PC

  • A native Rust desktop app. Capture, annotation and saving all happen locally.
  • Screenshot history is optional, off by default and stored only on your PC.
  • Window selection reads window geometry, not titles or contents.
  • Your R2 secret key is encrypted with Windows DPAPI.
  • No analytics, no usage statistics, no log files.
  • Text recognition (Ctrl+T) runs on your PC with Windows' built-in OCR: no network, no history entry, no text file written. The result window shows your image with a box around each line: click a box to copy that line, Ctrl+click to pick several. Windows has no Vietnamese recognizer yet, so accented letters may lose their marks.

When it uses the network

  • Only when you choose Upload: the image goes straight to your own Cloudflare R2 bucket over HTTPS. There is no intermediary upload server.
  • Optional update check (can be turned off): contacts GitHub and sends only the app name and version.

darkshot doesn't claim to be offline-only: uploads and update checks need a connection. Saving, copying, history and text recognition work without one.

    darkshot is currently unsigned

    darkshot is currently unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may display a warning on first launch. You can verify the downloaded file against the published SHA256 checksums.

    How to verify the download
    1. Download SHA256SUMS.txt from the same release.
    2. In PowerShell, run Get-FileHash .\darkshot.exe -Algorithm SHA256.
    3. Compare the hash with the line for that file in SHA256SUMS.txt. They must match exactly.
    4. If SmartScreen appears and the hash matches, choose More info → Run anyway.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered plainly.

    Is darkshot free?

    The source code is public on GitHub, and downloading a release doesn't ask for payment. The repository doesn't currently include a license file, so no license terms are published; check the repository for updates.

    Which Windows versions are supported?

    Windows 11, 64-bit.

    Do I need Cloudflare R2?

    No, not for saving locally or copying to the clipboard. R2 is required only for cloud uploads and shareable links.

    Does darkshot require installation?

    No traditional installer. Run the portable darkshot.exe directly.

    Where are my screenshots stored?

    Saved screenshots go to your save folder, by default Pictures\darkshot, and you can change it in Options. If you turn on history, its index and thumbnails live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\darkshot\history. A screenshot only exists in memory until you save or upload it.

    Are screenshots uploaded automatically?

    No. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose Upload (or use the optional quick-upload shortcut you enabled yourself). Uploads go only to the R2 bucket you configured.

    Can I customize keyboard shortcuts?

    Yes. The four capture shortcuts (region, quick save, quick upload, repeat last region) can be changed or turned off in Options → Hotkeys. Shortcuts inside the overlay, such as Ctrl+S, are fixed.

    Is darkshot available on macOS or Linux?

    No. darkshot is a Windows-only app and there are no macOS or Linux builds.

    Why does Windows SmartScreen show a warning?

    The executable is not yet digitally signed, so SmartScreen has no publisher reputation to check. Verify the file against SHA256SUMS.txt, then choose More info → Run anyway if you're comfortable.

    Can text recognition read Vietnamese?

    Not reliably yet. It uses the recognizers Windows provides, and Windows has no Vietnamese one, so accented letters may lose their marks. It still runs entirely on your PC, with no network.

    How can I verify the download?

    Each release includes SHA256SUMS.txt. Run Get-FileHash .\darkshot.exe -Algorithm SHA256 in PowerShell and compare the result with the matching line.
